How it works

1 · The "electrics" block

Creates smooth copies of the game's instant light values (like lowbeam) using a temporalNonLinear smoother. This is the official v0.34+ halogen system.

2 · Re-point your light props

In your car's lights jbeam, change each prop's function from "lowbeam" to "lowbeam_filament". Keep your own node names — only the first argument changes.

3 · The "lightScaling" block

Handles the color shift: strips green/blue at low power so the light starts dim-orange before reaching full warmth. Exactly how a filament behaves.

03 — Install

        
Three steps to warm light

      

    


    

      

        1
        
        
Extract your mod

        
Unzip your car into Documents/BeamNG.drive/mods/unpacked/ (working in unpacked makes iteration instant). Find the main jbeam and the jbeam containing your headlight props.

      

      

        2
        
        
Merge & re-point

        
Paste the "electrics" block into your main car jbeam (inside the big part object, next to "nodes"/"beams"). Then swap each light prop's function name and add the lightScaling block + warm lightColor.

      

      

        3
        
        
Reload & enjoy

        
In game, press Ctrl+R to reload the vehicle. Flip the lights at night and watch them breathe. If it's too slow or fast, nudge the smoother rates and reload.
